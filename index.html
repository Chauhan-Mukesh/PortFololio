01. About Me 👨‍💻

Hello! I'm Mukesh, a passionate software engineer with over 4 years of experience in the web development industry. My journey began with a deep curiosity for how things work on the internet, which led me to pursue a BCA and later an MSc in Information Technology.

Today, I have the privilege of working at Credencys Solutions Inc., where I lead implementation teams and architect full-stack solutions. My main focus is on the Pimcore framework, where I enjoy extending its capabilities to solve complex data management challenges for enterprise clients.
